Express Haulers Moving ("we", "us") provides residential and commercial moving services in New Jersey
and the surrounding area. This policy explains what personal information we collect, why we collect
it, and how you can ask us to delete it.

## Information we collect

**When you contact us for a quote or a move**, whether through our website, by phone, by text
message, by email, through Instagram, or through a listing service such as Yelp or Thumbtack, we
collect the details you give us:

- your name, phone number and email address
- the addresses you are moving from and to, and details about those properties such as floor,
  stairs, elevator and parking access
- your move date and the items to be moved
- messages you send us, and notes our team takes about your move

**When you call or text us**, we keep the call record (date, time, duration and phone number) and,
for calls that are recorded, the recording and a written summary. Our phone system tells callers
when a call is recorded.

**When you book a move**, we collect the information needed to carry it out and bill it, including
your deposit and payment details. Card payments are processed by Stripe; we do not store full card
numbers.

**When you message us on Instagram**, we receive the message you send, your Instagram name and
username, and the time it was sent. We use it only to answer you and to arrange your move.

## How we use your information

We use your information to answer your enquiry, prepare an estimate, schedule and carry out your
move, bill you, handle any claim or complaint, and keep records the law requires us to keep. We may
send you messages about your move, such as a confirmation before moving day. We do not sell your
personal information, and we do not use it for advertising by other companies.

## Automated tools

We use software to organise enquiries and to help our team keep track of them. This includes our
customer records system, our scheduling and dispatch tools, and assistive software that summarises
calls and messages for our own staff. These tools help our team respond; decisions about your move
are made by people.

## Who we share it with

We share information only with the service providers that help us run the business, and only as far
as they need it: our moving CRM (Movegistics), our project board (ClickUp), our phone and messaging
provider (RingCentral), our email provider (Google), our crew scheduling app (Connecteam), our
payment processor (Stripe), our team messaging tool (Slack) and our automation platform (n8n). We
also share information when the law requires it, or to establish or defend a legal claim.

## How long we keep it

We keep enquiry and move records for as long as we need them to run the business and to meet legal,
tax and insurance requirements, normally up to seven years. Call recordings and message summaries are
kept for a shorter period unless they relate to a claim.

## Text messages

You give us your mobile number when you contact us for a quote or book a move, and that is how we
text you about your enquiry, your estimate and your move. Messages are sent through our phone
provider, RingCentral.

**We do not sell or rent your mobile number, and we never share your mobile number or your consent to
be texted with third parties or affiliates for their own marketing or promotional purposes.** Your
number is shared only with the provider that delivers the message for us.

How often we text depends on your move: usually a handful of messages while we are arranging it.
Message and data rates may apply. Reply **STOP** to any message to stop receiving texts, or **HELP**
for help. Carriers are not liable for delayed or undelivered messages.

## Your choices

- **Text messages:** reply STOP to any text from us to stop receiving them.
- **Email:** ask us at any time to stop sending you email.
- **Access, correction and deletion:** see below.

## How to request deletion of your data

Email **contact@expresshaulers.com** with the subject line **"Data deletion request"**, or call
**(862) 379-9113**, and tell us the name, phone number or email address you used with us. We will
confirm your identity, delete the personal information we are not legally required to keep, and
reply to you within 30 days. If you contacted us on Instagram, tell us your Instagram username and
we will delete the messages we hold from that conversation.

## Children

Our services are meant for adults. We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone under
18. If you believe a minor has given us their information, contact us and we will delete it.

## Changes to this policy

If we change this policy we will update the date at the top of this page.
